# SERE Medicine, Climate, and Environments

Source: AFH 10-644, Chapters 5 (SERE Medicine), 6 (Weather), 7 (Environment), 8 (Global Climate Characteristics), 9 (Open Seas), 10 (Local People), 11 (Proper Body Temperature), 12 (Clothing), 13 (Shelter), 14 (Firecraft), 15 (Equipment).

## SERE medicine (Ch. 5)

Field medicine with no equipment, no evacuation, and no resupply, often while evading. Principles match the `first-aid` and `survival` skills; the SERE additions:

- **Prevention dominates:** foot care (blisters cripple evaders), hydration discipline, hygiene — dysentery and skin infections are the great killers of isolated personnel.
- Treat injuries early and completely — a small wound left dirty becomes a crippling infection in days.
- Know the quiet killers: internal bleeding, heatstroke, hypothermia, dehydration.

## Weather and climate (Ch. 6–8)

- Weather reading: clouds, wind, pressure changes, and what they predict.
- Global climate characteristics by region: arctic, desert, jungle, temperate, mountain, ocean — and what each demands (water discipline in the desert, warmth in the arctic, dryness in the jungle).
- Know the climate you are entering before you enter it; plan movement around it.

## Open seas (Ch. 9)

The raft; protection from sun and cold; water discipline (never drink seawater — collect rain, use the solar still); fishing; sharks; signaling. The sea kills by exposure and dehydration faster than by hunger — shade and water come first.

## Local people (Ch. 10)

The most important factor after the environment. They can feed you, hide you, guide you — or betray you. Observe before approaching; respect local customs; never assume friendliness; trade fairly. Local people have already solved the problems of this environment — watch what they eat, wear, and avoid.

## Body temperature and clothing (Ch. 11–12)

- Maintain temperature by insulation, activity, and shelter.
- Clothing is a system: layers, loose fit, dry, windproof outer, insulating inner.
- Cold: protect extremities and head. Heat: cover skin, wear a hat. Wet clothing is the enemy in every climate.

## Shelter, firecraft, equipment (Ch. 13–15)

| Topic | Key points |
|---|---|
| Shelter | Lean-to, debris hut, snow cave, raised platform, below-ground desert shelter — by environment; insulate from the ground first |
| Firecraft | Fire lays (tepee, log cabin, star); ignition (matches, ferro rod, lens, friction — bow drill most reliable); fire as signal and tool; three fires in a triangle = distress |
| Equipment | Core kit: cutting tool, fire starter, water container, cordage, signaling device, first-aid kit, navigation aids; maintain what you have, improvise the rest |
