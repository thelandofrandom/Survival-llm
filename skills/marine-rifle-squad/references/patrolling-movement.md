# Patrolling, Urban Operations, and Movement (MCRP 3-10A.4, Chapters 5–7, Appendices)

## Chapter outline

Ch. 5 Patrolling — Ch. 6 Military Operations on Urbanized Terrain — Ch. 7 Movement. Appendices A–H.

## Patrolling (squad level)

| Patrol type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Combat patrol | Accomplish a combat task |
| Ambush patrol | Surprise attack from concealment; planning, execution, withdrawal |
| Contact patrol | Establish contact with a friendly unit; exchange information |
| Reconnaissance patrol | Gather information (zone, area, route) without detection or decisive engagement |

- **Immediate actions upon enemy contact:** immediate action drills; danger areas; reactions to ambush, IED, air attack, drone (see `scouting_patrolling` for the full procedures).
- **Patrol base operations:** planning considerations; occupation; base routine (security, rest, resupply, hygiene); depart quietly, leaving no sign.

## Urban operations at squad level

- **Initiative-based tactics:** small units in a city must act on initiative because communications and control will fail.
- **Ad hoc organization:** reorganize the squad for the urban task (assault, support, security elements).
- **Exterior movement:** danger areas (streets, intersections, open ground); satellite patrolling; cordon and search; ladder employment; overwatch.
- **RIGS cycle:** Reconnoiter → Isolate → Gain a foothold → Seize → Consolidate and Reorganize.
- **Interior movement:** individual movement in buildings; commands; clearing procedures — room by room, floor by floor, top-down (see `mout` for the full clearing drills).
- **Defensive considerations:** strongpoints, fighting positions in buildings.
- **Stability actions:** tactical callouts (loudspeakers and interpreters to communicate with civilians and call occupants out of a building); checkpoints.

## Movement

| Mode | Key points |
|---|---|
| Mounted (general) | Vehicles provide firepower, mobility, protection; squad provides security, dismounted reconnaissance, clearing terrain vehicles cannot enter |
| HMMWV | Organization for riding; operations; dismount drills; immediate action and battle drills (ambush, IED, rollover) |
| Mechanized | Maneuver with armored vehicles; AAV as armored personnel carrier; tank/infantry integration — tanks provide firepower and shock, infantry provides security and close combat, neither works without the other |
| Air assault | Speed and surprise; dependence on aircraft and weather; air movement table, landing plan, ground tactical plan; load, ride, and dismount quickly and in order; fight immediately on landing |
