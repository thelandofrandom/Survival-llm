# Fighting Tips and Arab Customs — CALL: Winning in the Desert (1990)

Distilled from CALL: *Winning in the Desert*, DTIC ADA446544, August 1990 — the Tips on Fighting in the Desert chapter and the Knowledge of Arab Customs chapter.

## Tips on Fighting in the Desert

**Intelligence:** allow recon lead times; confirm ~80% of enemy anti-tank systems to 6-digit accuracy; scouts are reconnaissance, not combat; visibility is limited.

**Maneuver:** terrain masking is the only cover; shadows betray positions from the air; dig in; move as the sun moves; shade optics; stake the trigger point; range estimation is distorted; inspect your position from the enemy's point of view; light and noise carry for miles; move at night; sand reveals movement.

**Fire support:** hot ammunition affects ballistics; dig in ammo storage; use illumination and smoke to reorient; the FSC plans all fires.

**Mobility, countermobility, survivability:** linear obstacles; minefields can be laid rapidly over large areas; the enemy attacks with the sun low behind him; two operators per piece of engineer equipment; a tank platoon dug in two-step positions can destroy a battalion; basic loads of Class IV with all vehicles; reverse slopes; dummy positions; MOPP; chemical agents — heat increases liquid-agent incapacitation, wind evaporates and disperses agents.

**Air defense:** multinational airspace SOPs; host-nation liaisons to the BCE; sunlight affects Stinger.

**CSS:** medical distances are long; the battlefield is nonlinear and intermingled; at least one combat lifesaver per squad; medics must recertify; weapons cleaned constantly; PMCS is essential; food service needs fresh fruit, vegetables, and breads.

**C2:** operate where contact is maintained with forward units and the TOC; EENT/BMNT and illumination matter; FM dead spots come from minerals; know the commander's intent and rehearse the battle drill.

## Knowledge of Arab Customs

- Alcohol and pornography are prohibited in theater.
- Shake hands on meeting and leaving.
- Never expose the sole of your shoe.
- Don't ask about women.
- Friendship is measured by duration and intensity.
- Never give a flat "no."
- Same-sex touching and holding hands are acceptable; opposite-sex touching and kissing in public are obscene.
- Stand close.
- Don't be impatient — a late arrival is an insult.
- No direct public criticism: take a man aside privately.
- Don't patronize.
- No photos of installations, police, airports, or seaports, or close-range photos of people — especially women — without permission.
- Don't stare at or strike up conversation with Arab women in public.
