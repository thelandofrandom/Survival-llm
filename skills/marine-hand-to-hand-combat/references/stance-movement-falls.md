# Stance, Movement, Falls — Marine Hand-to-Hand Combat (Sections 1–3)

## Principles and vulnerable targets (¶101–104)

Hand-to-hand fighting matters when weapons are unavailable or impractical, "particularly when silence is desirable." Seven principles: be aggressive; keep eyes on the opponent; distract the opponent; disable or be disabled; vary the attack to fit the situation; turn the defense into an unrelenting attack; feel superior to the opponent regardless of size or strength.

**Vulnerable, vital, or sensitive areas (¶103):** arch of the foot, knee, groin, temple, ears, nerves under the ears, solar plexus, throat, bridge of the nose, elbow, collarbone, eyes, neck, side of the jaw, base of the spine, small of the back, kidneys. The fingers, wrist, and elbow are the easiest bones to break or dislocate. Train with care — do not accidentally injure or immobilize a training partner.

## The offensive-defensive stance (¶201)

Left foot forward, toe slightly inward, left knee slightly bent inward; right foot to the rear and right of the left, toe straight forward, knee slightly bent; lean forward from the hips, weight balanced on both feet. Left hand guards the left face/head, left forearm and elbow guard the left body; right hand and forearm do the same on the right. Palms inward, lower edge of the hands toward the opponent, fingers together, thumbs tight against the forefingers. From this position the fighter is prepared to strike or grasp.

## Movement (¶202)

All steps are about 14 inches and preserve the stance: forward (left foot, right follows); left-and-forward (left foot, pivot on ball of right); right-and-forward (right foot, pivot on ball of left); left-and-to-the-rear (right foot back-left, pivot on ball of left); right-and-to-the-rear (left foot back-left, pivot on ball of right); directly to the rear (right foot back, left follows). Never cross the feet.

## Falls and rolls (¶301–302)

**Forward roll:** two steps for momentum, dive, chin against left shoulder, left hand at left side of and slightly above the head, right arm slightly bent to break the fall with hand and forearm; roll forward on the right shoulder and back; on foot contact, spin left to face the rear, in stance.

**Backward fall:** drop back onto the buttocks as in sitting, breaking the shock with hands and forearms; head on chest; raise feet and swing them over the head in a backward somersault when the hands touch the ground; land on knees or feet facing the opponent, in stance immediately. Both end in the fighting stance, facing the opponent.
