# Breaking Holds and Weapons — Marine Hand-to-Hand Combat (Sections 6–8)

## Breaking holds (¶601–609)

One method for nine holds: grip the attacking limb, unbalance the opponent with a pivot or twist, break the hold, finish with a blow to a vital target.

- **¶601 — front wrist hold (two-handed):** pull back with the right hand, step forward with the left foot, push forward and upward with the left hand; grasp his left elbow with the left hand, shift right and forward; finish with a blow to temple, jaw, neck, or kidney.
- **¶602 — rear neck hold:** grasp his right elbow with the right hand and his left elbow with the left; pull down with the left, up with the right, pivoting left as he is forced to twist down and over; follow with a knee blow or throw.
- **¶603 — rear choke hold:** grasp his left hand or wrist with the left hand, pivot left retaining the grip; finish with a blow to temple, jaw, or throat. (Reverse if he uses his left hand.)
- **¶604 — front choke hold:** grasp his right wrist/hand with the left hand, shift left and pivot on the right foot, forcing his hand off and down to the right; finish with a blow to temple, jaw, or throat.
- **¶605 — crossed-wrist front choke hold:** grasp his right hand/wrist with the right hand, shift left and forward, pull down with the right hand and lift up with the left hand on his right elbow; finish with a knee to the kidney or a blow to the neck.
- **¶606 — rear head lock:** grasp his left elbow with the left hand and his left knee with the right; step forward and left, pull right and upward, forcing him off balance and down on his side; finish with a blow to temple or throat.
- **¶607 — front head lock:** slide the right hand and arm over his right elbow with the left hand; push down with the right shoulder, pivot left to break the hold; follow with a throw, a blow to the groin, or break his right arm.
- **¶608 — hammerlock:** step forward with the right foot, bend forward from the waist, rapidly twist left to break the hold; finish with a blow to temple, jaw, or kidney.
- **¶609 — come-along (finger-bending or hand-upward holds):** lift upward with the free hand against his hand(s) while driving the right knee into the back of his left knee, forcing him to break the hold and knocking him on his back; follow with a knee or a blow to the bridge of the nose or throat. (If he holds the left hand/arm, use the left knee against his left knee.)

## Disarming a bayonet-armed opponent (¶701–703)

- **¶701 — slow approach, bayonet low:** sidestep, reach forward and down with the left hand, hit the weapon on the inside and deflect the bayonet left with a pushing movement; shift right and forward, strike temple, neck, or left elbow.
- **¶702 — slow approach, bayonet high:** reach out with the left hand, hit the weapon and push it left; quickly shift right and forward, grasp the rifle near the stock with the right hand and wrest it away; finish with a blow to elbow, temple, or neck.
- **¶703 — charging opponent:** shift left and forward to avoid the bayonet, hit and push the weapon downward and to the right with the left hand; finish with a blow to the neck. Never meet the point — step off its line, deflect the weapon, strike the man.

## Knife and club fighting (¶801–803)

- **¶801 — frontal attack with the knife:** offensive-defensive stance, knife in the right hand, point toward the opponent, blade flat, cutting edge outward; advance with the left hand high to protect the face or strike. If he grasps the knife hand, strike the bridge of the nose; shift left, place the left hand under his right elbow and lift the arm, plunge the knife upward into the body above the belt, slash outward or to the right.
- **¶802 — frontal disarming, knife or club:** (a) against a thrust, shift forward and left, deflect the knife by pushing it with the left hand, strike the back of his hand with the outer edge of the right hand, follow with an arm break or a blow to jaw, throat, or collarbone; (b) step in with the left foot, strike the inside of his right wrist with the left hand/wrist to deflect the thrust left, then strike the bridge of the nose with the right hand and simultaneously knee the groin; follow by breaking his arm or throwing him.
- **¶803 — attack from the rear:** (a) with a knife, place a hand over his mouth and face and pull back, plunging the knife upward into his back above the belt; (b) with a club, place the left hand on his arm or shoulder, turn him slightly left, and deliver the blow(s) to the base of the skull. This is sentry-removal material — a battlefield technique, not a self-defense syllabus.
