# Deflecting, Striking, Throws — Marine Hand-to-Hand Combat (Sections 4–5)

## Deflecting and striking blows (¶401–405)

Pattern: deflect, grasp, strike a vital target, often throw. Signature strike: the back edge of the hand to the temple, throat, jaw, or base of the skull; signature follow-up: the knee to the groin.

- **¶401 — right hand blow:** deflect right with left hand/forearm, grasp the wrist with the left hand, pull the opponent forward, strike temple/throat/side of neck with the back edge of the right hand.
- **¶402 — left hand blow:** deflect left with right hand/forearm, grasp the left arm with the left hand, shift forward and right, strike temple/jaw/base of skull with the back edge of the right hand, using the shift's momentum.
- **¶403 — wide swinging right (roundhouse):** block with left hand/forearm, grasp the right wrist/arm with the left hand, pull forward, knee the groin and strike between the eyes with the back edge of the right hand simultaneously; pivot left, throwing the opponent over the right thigh or knee.
- **¶404 — attacking an opponent:** against a threatened left-hand blow, strike the outside of his left wrist with the left hand, shift right and forward, strike temple/jaw/base of skull with the right hand.
- **¶405 — deflecting kicks:** against a right-foot kick, shift forward and left, deflect the kick right and upward with the left hand, swing/raise the leg upward, knocking him on his back — then break the leg, kick the groin, or deliver a karate blow to the throat or bridge of the nose. Against a left-foot kick, shift right and do the same.

## Throws and take-downs (¶501–503)

- **¶501 — from the opponent's left:** grasp his left wrist with the left hand, step in with the right foot, drive the right knee into the outside of his left knee while striking the temple with the outer edge of the right hand; place the right hand on his face, push him backward over the right knee/thigh and drop him; finish with a blow to the bridge of the nose or throat.
- **¶502 — from the left, alternate:** grasp the left wrist, step forward with the right foot, knee the outside of the left knee, pivot under his left arm, grasp the inside of his left leg at the knee, drive the elbow into the solar plexus; lift with the right hand, release the wrist as he falls backward; follow with a knee to his left side and a right-hand blow to the throat or bridge of the nose.
- **¶503 — from the opponent's right:** grasp his right wrist with the left hand, raise it above the head while bending forward, drive the shoulder into his solar plexus while stepping forward with the right foot; as he leans forward, grasp his right leg at the knee, raise him and swing him over the shoulder, retaining the wrist grip; once down, strike temple, throat, or bridge of the nose.

Every take-down is a knee strike and a face strike built into the throw, ending with the opponent down and the fighter delivering a finishing blow.
