# Appendices — ATP 3-90.99 (2021)

## Appendix A — Desert regions of the world

A survey of the world's desert regions: classification, climate, terrain, and military significance. The reference for planning an operation in an unfamiliar desert rather than the one you trained in.

## Appendix B — Techniques for operating equipment in the desert

Desert-specific equipment techniques: sand and dust protection, lubrication and cleaning intervals, cooling and battery management, and the maintenance burden desert conditions impose. Practical companion to Chapter 1's equipment section: maintenance time in the desert is combat power — a weapon or vehicle that fails from sand is a casualty the enemy did not have to inflict.

## Appendix C — Techniques for fratricide reduction in the desert

Long engagement ranges, excellent observation, and featureless terrain make fratricide a special desert risk. Covers target identification, coordination measures, and the discipline of positive identification at ranges where the enemy and a friendly unit look identical through a sight.

## Appendix D — Marine Corps doctrinal schema conversions

Converts Marine Corps doctrinal terms and structures to Army equivalents so the joint publication reads cleanly for both services.
