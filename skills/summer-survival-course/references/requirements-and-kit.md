# Requirements and Kit — MWTC Summer Survival (2002), Chapters 1–2

## Requirements for survival (Chapter 1)

- **Will to survive / attitude:** the precondition — "if you do not have the right attitude, you may not survive."
- **SURVIVAL acronym:** Size up the situation / surroundings / physical condition / equipment; Undue haste makes waste; Remember where you are; Vanquish fear and panic; Improvise; Value living; Act like the natives; Learn basic skills.
- **Survival stressors:** injury, illness, death; uncertainty and lack of control; environment; hunger and thirst; fatigue; isolation. Name the stressor, counter it with a task.
- **Priorities of work:** First 24 hours (critical) — shelter, fire, water, signaling, in the order enemy/weather/terrain/time of day/resources dictate. Second 24 hours — tools and weapons, traps and snares, pathguards (noise and casualty-producing guards on avenues of approach). Remainder — continuously improve until rescue.
- **Group survival:** depends on organizing activity; emergencies do not initially unite a group. High morale comes from internal cohesiveness.

## Survival kit (Chapter 2)

- **Layer it:** most important items on the body; body kit is much smaller than a vehicle kit. Environment dictates contents; choose multipurpose items.
- **Case:** water-repellent/waterproof, easy to carry or attach to the body, accepts various sized items, durable (first aid case, ammo pouch, or similar).
- **Six components:** (1) fire starting — matches, magnifying glass, flint and steel, lighter, potassium permanganate with sugar or antifreeze, prepackaged tinder (commercial or cotton balls + petroleum jelly); (2) water procurement — iodine tablets/Betadine/iodine solution, metal container for boiling (canteen cup), water-carrying items; (3) food procurement — hooks, sinkers, leaders, swivels, jigs, snares, 550 cord for gill net/trap construction; (4) signaling — mirror, pyrotechnics (smoke, pen flares), lights (flashlight, strobe, chemlight); (5) first aid; (6) shelter — cord and water-repellent material (poncho, tarp, parachute).
