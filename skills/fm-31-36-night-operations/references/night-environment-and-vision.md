# The Night Environment and Vision — FM 31-36 (TEST)

## Illumination Decides Courses of Action

Illumination levels at night directly affect the selection of courses of action. Night operations should be conducted when light levels provide maximum advantage to friendly units with night vision devices. Passive night vision devices (no artificial illumination) increase night vision under most illumination levels; under very low levels, infrared, pink, or visible illumination can be used with them.

Clear nights let units equipped with night vision devices operate with near-day efficiency against forces not equally equipped. When the capability of two forces is equal, relatively good night visibility favors the defender, by permitting him to observe the approach of the attacker. Vegetation and terrain giving concealment from ground and air observation normally favor an attacker, whether or not the defender has night viewing devices.

## The Numbers of the Night

- **Full moon:** about **one-hundredth of a foot-candle** of illumination (40° North Latitude).
- **Quarter moon:** about **one-thousandth of a foot-candle**.
- **Moonless night sky:** a comparatively low but significant source of illumination.
- **Full moon levels** prevail only **about 30 percent of the time** at middle latitudes in July; light from the moonless night sky with reduced levels can be expected **approximately 25 percent of the time**.
- The moon approximates a point source of illumination; passive night vision devices greatly multiply existing illumination and are very effective under moonlight.
- **Overcast** reduces light levels by a **factor of 2 for thin overcast to a factor of 10 for dark, heavy storm clouds**.

## Dark Adaptation

- Dark vision — the ability to see unusually well under very reduced illumination — is achieved by allowing little or no light to enter the eye for approximately **30 minutes**, during which chemical changes in the eye make it unusually sensitive to small amounts of light.
- Dark vision is rapidly destroyed by exposure to normal and bright light, so as a practical matter the individual soldier on the battlefield cannot acquire and preserve maximum dark-adapted vision — except infrequently, as on patrols where burning and exploding incidents are not encountered.
- When not equipped with night vision devices, practice **off-center vision and scanning observation** — off-center vision is more sensitive at low illumination levels.
- Avoid looking at all light sources if possible.

## Length of the Night

Anywhere on earth throughout the year, exactly one-half of all hours are hours of darkness — but the distribution, and the length of twilight (between the beginning of morning civil twilight and the end of evening civil twilight), vary with latitude and season. The manual provides figures for planning by latitude and month.
