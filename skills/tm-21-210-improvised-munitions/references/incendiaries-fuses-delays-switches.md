**Doctrine:** gelled flame fuels are the section's core — gasoline gelled with a common agent sticks to the target and burns longer than liquid fuel. Fire-bottle items pair a gelled or liquid fuel with a chemical or mechanical igniter and a delay; the acid delay incendiary uses the time for acid to eat through a barrier as its delay.

## Section VI — Fuses, Detonators, and Delay Mechanisms

The initiation train — how to set off the items in Sections I–V. The firing train is blasting cap → booster → main charge, and the manual's initiators are the means of firing the cap.

| Item | Principle |
|---|---|
| Electric bulb initiator | Light-bulb filament as an electric initiator |
| Fuse igniter from book matches | Match-head igniter for fuse |
| Delay igniter from cigarette | A cigarette as a time-delay igniter |
| Watch delay timer | A wristwatch as a delay timer |
| Dried seed timer | A seed-swelling delay timer |
| Fast burning fuse | Improvised time fuse |
| Slow burning fuse | Improvised time fuse |
| Clothespin time delay switch | A clothespin as an electrical time-delay switch |
| Can-liquid time delay | A liquid-level time delay |
| Short-term time delay for grenade | Delay fuze for grenades |
| Long-term time delay for grenade | Delay fuze for grenades |
| Mousetrap switch | Spring-driven switch |
| Pull-loop switch | Trip/pull switch |
| Knife switch | Manual switch |
| Altimeter switch | Pressure/altitude switch |
| Improvised scale | Field-expedient scale for weighing charges |
| Rope grenade launching technique | Rope technique for launching grenades |

**Doctrine:** any measurable process can be a delay — a burning cigarette, a swelling seed, a draining liquid, a watch movement, or a clothespin held by a dissolving or burning element. Switches are the triggers: pressure, pull, release, tilt, altitude, or manual action closing an electrical circuit or releasing a spring.

**Doctrine:** the electrical items (generators, batteries) power the firing switches and initiators of Section VI, and the armor table tells the operator what his improvised weapons can and cannot defeat.
