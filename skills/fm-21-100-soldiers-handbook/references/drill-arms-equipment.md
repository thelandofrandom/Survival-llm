# Drill, Arms, Equipment — FM 21-100 (Chapters 5–9, ¶¶54–184)

## Clothing (Chapter 5, ¶¶54–59)

Issued on an allowance basis and accounted for by the soldier. Keep it clean, mended, and stored properly; repair tears and loose buttons promptly; wear the uniform complete, clean, and correctly adjusted — in uniform, the soldier represents the Army to every civilian who sees him.

## Arms and Equipment (Chapter 6, ¶¶60–85)

- **Responsibility (¶¶60–62):** the soldier is personally responsible for his arms and equipment; neglect is a disciplinary matter and a practical failure.
- **U.S. rifle, caliber .30, M1 (¶63):** nomenclature, care and cleaning — disassembly as authorized, clean bore and chamber, light oiling, keep free of dirt and moisture. Clean after firing and after exposure to rain or dust; inspect before use.
- **Automatic pistol, caliber .45, M1911 (¶64):** nomenclature, care and cleaning, safety devices.
- **Safety precautions (¶65):** treat every weapon as loaded; never point it at anything you do not intend to shoot; finger off the trigger until ready to fire; know what is beyond the target.
- **Gas mask (¶¶66–72):** fitting, wearing, care, and drill — a central concern in 1940, when gas warfare was a live expectation.
- **Field equipment and pack (¶¶73–84):** pack, haversack, canteen, mess kit, entrenching tool, shelter half; how the infantry pack is made up and carried; packing individual equipment on horseback; formal display (layout) of equipment for inspection.

## Drill (Chapters 7–9, ¶¶86–184)

- **Without arms (Ch. 7, ¶¶86–102):** positions (attention, rest, parade rest), facings, steps and marchings.
- **With arms (Ch. 8, ¶¶103–151):** manual of arms for the rifle, loadings and firings, carrying the automatic rifle, manual of the pistol.
- **Squad and platoon (Ch. 9, ¶¶152–184):** squad drill (¶¶152–168), platoon drill (¶¶169–184) — formations, movements, commands. The point of drill is that a unit which can move together on the drill field can move together under fire.
