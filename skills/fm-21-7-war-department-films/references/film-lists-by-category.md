# Film Lists by Category — FM 21-7 (Section IV, ¶¶16–24)

Lists give serial number, year released, title, running time (minutes). Asterisk (*) = authorized for ASF and AGF training and permanent retention by film libraries. Running times garbled in text extraction are not guessed here — check the full text or the PDF.

## Training Films (¶16, TF)

Largest list (manual pp. 13–44), ordered by branch prefix: 1- Air Corps/Air Forces; 7- infantry/dismounted drill; 8- medical/hygiene; 11- signal/communications; 17- armored force; 19- military police/personnel; 21- general military training; 30- military intelligence; other prefixes through the 40s. Examples: TF 1-133 Modern Weather Theory — Primary Circulation (1940, 19 min); 1-134 Modern Weather Theory — Development and Characteristics of Atmospheric Waves (1940, 14 min); 1-136 Aircraft Engines — Elements of Electricity as Applied to Ignition Systems (1941, 28 min); 1-222 Small Arms Ammunition — .30 Cal., .50 Cal., and .45 Cal. (1942, 9 min); 1-294 Identification of Aircraft — Messerschmitt 109 (1942, 8 min); 1-326 Aerial Navigation — Dead Reckoning (1942, 26 min); 1-438 Identification of Aircraft — Spitfire (1942, 5 min).

## Foreign Versions (¶17, IT)

Italian-rescored films for Italian Service Units: IT 165 Principles of Oxy-acetylene Welding and Cutting; IT 200–203 Pioneer Equipment (wood cutting tools, manila rope, hitches, knots and bends); IT 235 Articles of War; IT 551–559 The Motor Vehicle Driver series; IT 1238 Sex Hygiene; IT 2048 Military Courtesy (all 1944).

## Film Bulletins (¶18, FB)

New developments, not necessarily approved doctrine; several short items often share one number. FB 1: The 90-mm Antiaircraft Gun; The 37-mm Remote Control Antiaircraft Gun; The 16-inch Howitzer (Firing Tests). FB 2: A New Type Trench Digger; Experimental Type Flame Thrower; Rubber Boats. FB 3: Experimental Carriers for 37-mm Gun; The 75-mm New Gun Half-Track Carrier; Experimental Incendiary Bomb.

## Miscellaneous and MIS Films (¶¶19–20)

- **Miscellaneous (¶19):** special productions; adopted films from other services.
- **MIS Japanese-language (¶20, special distribution):** Misc. 526 Vow in the Desert; 585 Prayer at Dawn; 1013 Atarashi Kazoku; 1043 Earth and Soldier; 1051 Mother (all 1944).

## Combat Bulletins (¶21, CB) and Combat Reports (¶22, CR)

Numbered combat newsreels with contents described in the catalogue. CB 1 Combat Shots, Pacific and Italian Theaters (1944, 13 min); CB 2 Bridging the Volturno River, Italy; Landings in the South Pacific; Invasion of Cape Gloucester (14 min); CB 3 Building Pill Box in Italy; Battlefield Concealment; Operations at Los Negros; Embarkation for Anzio (20 min); CB 4 The Battle for Cassino; Japs and Jungle; Tanks and Infantry Face Enemy Fire (19 min); CB 5 Stop That Tank; Rear Area in India; The B-29 (16 min); CB 6 Beachhead Operations — A Day on an European Beachhead (23 min); CB 13 Capture of Saipan; Seized Luftwaffe Films (17 min); CB 14 Allied Drive Breaks Nazi Normandy Defenses; Capture of Leghorn (16 min); CB 15 Activities in North Burma; Battle of France; Invasion of Guam (29 min); CB 17 Invasion of Southern France (38 min); CB 22 Yanks in Germany; British Take Antwerp; Buzz Bombs Halted (26 min); CB 27 Clean-up on Peleliu and Angaur; Landings on Leyte (28 min). CB 13 onward also distributed as SFR for domestic distribution. CR 1 Liberation of Rome (1944, 21 min) — further distribution requires special authorization.

## Information & Education and Public Relations (¶¶23–24)

- **I&E (¶23):** War Information Films — "Why We Fight," "Know Your Enemy," "Know Your Ally" — shown at post theaters in daytime hours, not as evening entertainment; 16-mm and 35-mm; later available through service command film libraries. Other I&E subjects listed by issue number, year, running time.
- **Bureau of Public Relations (¶24):** films listed by number and title.

## Subject Index

The manual closes with a subject index of films and film strips — the fastest entry when you know the subject but not the serial number. Examples: "Abandon ship, procedures, precautions, and safety measures — TF 21-1244"; "Aerial cableways, construction — FS 7-140"; "Aerodynamics of gliders — FS 1-91"; "Aerial bombs (see Bombs, Aerial)"; "AC mechanical fuel pumps (see Pumps, Fuel)."
