# Pay, Allowances, LES, Travel — TC 21-7 (Chapters 1–4)

## Pay, allowances, deductions (Chapter 1)

Paid by SURE PAY direct deposit; active-duty soldiers may elect once- or twice-monthly pay. Covered: base pay, BAH, BAS, CONUS/OCONUS COLA, FSA, OHA, clothing allowance, per diem, special pays (enlistment bonuses, imminent danger/hostile fire pay, flight pay, sea pay, hardship duty pay).

**2003 figures:** BAS $242.81/month when authorized to mess separately; $262.50/month when rations-in-kind are not available. Clothing allowance $274.01 (TDY 15 days in a 30-day period); $548.02 (30 days in a 36-month period). Imminent danger/hostile fire pay $150/month; hardship duty pay $150/month.

**Deductions:** federal tax on entitlements ending in "PAY"; state tax where applicable; BAH not taxable. Also AAFES deferred-payment charges overdue; 50¢/month (Regular Army enlisted and warrant officers) for the Soldiers' and Sailors' Home; SGLI up to $20/month for up to $250,000 coverage (2003); SSLI for National Guard; fines/forfeitures (Article 15, court-martial); garnishments (alimony, child support, IRS, commercial debts — via DFAS Cleveland after legal action); Government Property Lost or Damaged; bad checks at government agencies plus $25–$35 return-check fee (2003); Montgomery G.I. Bill.

**Allotments:** designated portion of pay to a qualified allottee; continues through deployment, leave, TDY, hospital. Maximum **six discretionary allotments**; AER, CFC, American Red Cross, government-debt repayment, and savings bonds do not count. Started through the PAC. Types: bank, voluntary support, mortgage/rent, non-individual (mutual funds, insurance), charitable, loan repayment, authorized purchases (savings bonds). Allotments stop on separation — plan ahead.

**TSP:** federal retirement savings plan, 401(k)-like; extended to servicemembers by NDAA FY2001. Enroll via TSP-U-1 through S-1; verify contributions on the LES and semiannual statements; penalties for early withdrawal; for short-term saving use U.S. Savings Bonds instead. www.tsp.gov (as cited 2003).

**myPay:** print LESs; direct-deposit, allotment, and address changes; TSP options; savings bonds; travel vouchers; tax withholdings.

## AC entitlements and the LES (Chapter 2)

AC and AGR soldiers, paid on DMO-AC. Scenarios by status: single SSG and below in barracks with government meals (base pay, BAS, partial BAH); single SFC and above electing off-post quarters (base pay, BAH without-dependents rate, BAS); with dependents in government quarters (base pay, BAS) or off-post (base pay, BAH with-dependents rate, BAS); officers, single and with families; dual-military couples (each base pay, BAS, special pays; one claims dependents for BAH with-dependents rate off-post).

**The LES:** walked block by block (fields 1–24+): entitlements, deductions, allotments, summary; mid-month Net Pay Advice (NPA) shows the mid-month payment. Read every LES and verify. PCS entitlements: travel, home allowances, dislocation allowance, temporary lodging expense.

## RC entitlements and mobilization (Chapter 3)

**RC pay system:** Defense Military Pay Office–Reserve Component (DMO-RC), pays up to eight times per month; RC soldiers paid on the next scheduled payday after duty. The soldier holds the most current pay information. LES issued for duty performed and on any MMPA administrative change; read each one carefully. Pay problems while deployed: use the chain of command at the lowest level.

**Mobilization:** ordered only by the President and/or Congress; directed by the Secretary of Defense. Prepare pay records during IDT, annual training, and at the mobilization site.

**Protections:** Soldiers' and Sailors' Civil Relief Act (SSCRA, 1940) — renamed the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA) later in 2003; National Committee for Employer Support of the Guard and Reserve (reemployment problems after mobilization).

## Travel claims (Chapter 4)

**DD Form 1351-2, Travel Voucher** — both components, different pay channels (PAC for AC, unit administrator for RC). File **within 5 days** of completing travel; traveler signs block 21a; attach orders and original lodging receipts; **receipts required for reimbursable expenses of $75.00 or more**; supervisor reviews and signs. Entitlements: mileage, commercial transportation (not exceeding GTR cost), per diem, other authorized expenses.

**"Lodging Plus" per diem:** M&IE **$26–$38/day (2003)**, **75% on first and last travel days**; lodging reimbursed at actual single-room cost up to the area rate. Government dining/billeting expected when available, reimbursed at the government rate. Parking and car rental are separate reimbursable expenses, not per diem.
