# Abbreviations — FM 21-30 (Sections V–VI, ¶¶10–13)

## General Abbreviations (Section V, ¶¶10–11)

General abbreviations approved for use on maps — place names, geographic features, and map marginalia (e.g., Sec. for Section). Paragraph 11 gives the abbreviations used with hydrographic symbols. These belong to the terrain on the map, as distinct from the military abbreviations of Section VI, which belong in orders and reports.

## Military Abbreviations — Rules (¶12)

Authorized for combat orders, messages, records, and reports in the field. With few exceptions all are written **without spacing and periods**. Dates abbreviated: **1 Jan 24**. Usually a single word is a single capital letter, or a capital and a lower-case letter.

**Staff sections:** G-1 personnel, G-2 military intelligence, G-3 operations and training, G-4 supply (general staff); S-1 adjutant, S-2 intelligence officer, S-3 plans and training officer, S-4 supply officer (brigade level and below, including battalion).

## Worked Examples (¶13)

Paragraph 13 shows the abbreviations in use in orders and messages, combined with the symbols of Section IV: number + branch abbreviation + echelon mark builds a unit designation; dates take the 1 Jan 24 form. Decode symbol and abbreviation together — the symbol says what and where, the abbreviation says which unit and what it is doing.
