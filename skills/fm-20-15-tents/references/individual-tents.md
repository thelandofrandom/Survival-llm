# Individual Tents — FM 20-15 (Chapter 1, ¶¶1–11)

## Shelter Tent (¶¶1–3)

- **Issue:** one shelter half per man with pins and poles; two men carry and occupy one complete tent. New type has triangular end pieces closing the tent completely — superior in cold and against wind and rain. Also usable as a fly for cooking cover. Individual responsibility, carried in the field pack.
- **Old type (¶2):** inverted-V back, open front; 33-in. wide, 7.9-oz cotton duck treated with aluminum acetate, wax, and soap; two buttoned sections; ridge height 43 in.; ventilation by opening the closed end. One man erects in ~7 minutes.
- **Formal pitching (¶2b):** ground for concealment and comfort beats alignment except at inspections. Odd-numbered man marks front pole position with left heel; pairs pitch together. Rifle laid muzzle-front, barrel-left; packs two paces front; halves spread buttons-to-center, triangle-to-rear, even man's half on the left, one half right-side-out and the other inside-out (right side stamped "U.S."). Button together; odd man holds front pole upright at the pin; even man pins front corner, drives front guy pin one tent-pole length ahead, ties guy line with pole vertical; rear pole set, rear guy pin two and one-half tent-pin lengths from the rear triangle pin; remaining pins — even man left, odd man right.
- **Double shelter tent (¶2b(3)):** two old-type tents buttoned at square ends, three poles; better in cold/inclement weather and restricted areas. Four halves buttoned ridge-first, then square ends; center eyelets stack lower-front, lower-rear, upper-front, upper-rear. Two guy pins each end, one pole-length from corner pins. Only old type — new type has built-in double ends.
- **Striking (¶2c):** unbutton to grasp poles, let them fall left or right, pull pins, unbutton halves, roll packs.
- **New type (¶3, Change 1, 1947):** added V-section front end; ventilate by opening one or both ends. Pitched as old type except both halves right side out, and front guy pin two and one-half tent-pin lengths from the front triangle pin.

## Mountain Tent and Jungle Hammock (¶¶4–11)

- **Two-man mountain tent:** complete unit with pins and poles, built-in cloth floor — desirable in extreme cold, keeping out wind and drifting snow.
- **Jungle hammock:** individual jungle shelter, off the ground against flooding and ground insects.
- Both pitch on the same principles: level ground, taut lines, guyed poles, ventilation.
