# Wall and General-Purpose Tents — FM 20-15 (Chapters 2–3, ¶¶12–60)

## Wall Tents (Chapter 2)

- **Types:** fire-resistant wall small O-D, wall large O-D, storage O-D — olive-drab treated cotton duck, issued complete with pins, poles, and lines per the manual's parts lists with stock numbers.
- **Pitching (¶18):** ~20 minutes for four men (large and small wall); 30 minutes for eight men (storage). Officer/NCO selects ground, facing direction, line, and door-pin position. Sequence: pin to mark door center → spread tent, door foot stops over door pin → front corners taut, aligned, pinned → lace rear door → rear corners taut both ways, pinned → four eave-line pins each corner in prolongation of the diagonals, ~two paces beyond corner pins → loosen front door → loosen two corner foot stops on one side only → ridge pole between ventilation strip and ridge → upright spindles into ridge pole and grommets → raise and hold → replace loosened foot stops, tighten guy lines to hold poles vertical → wall pins through foot stops, then intermediate eave-line pins in alignment.
- **Striking (¶19):** remove all pins except the four corner eave-line pins and down-wind corner wall pins; unfasten guy lines and hold while lowering — lower down wind; remove poles and remaining pins; fasten poles together; collect pins.
- **Folding (¶20):** flat at the ridge, side-wall bottoms even, sod cloth folded under, ends as triangles; wall bottom folded up ~1 ft; triangular ends in to a rectangle; top over ~9 in.; fold top-to-foot twice; lines onto the tent except the second eave line from each end; ends in to cover two-thirds of the second panel; left fold doubled until 3–5 in. from the right fold; right fold on top; tie with the two exposed eave lines. Side walls, not the top, end up exposed.

## General-Purpose Tents (Chapter 3)

- **Squad, M-1942, O-D** (6-ft 2-in. door); **pyramidal, M-1934, O-D**; **command post, M-1942, O-D**; **hospital ward, O-D**; **assembly, O-D** (sectional, sections overlapping ~3 ft for depth); **maintenance shelter**; **surgical truck, O-D**; **screen, latrine, fire-resistant, O-D**.
- Each section gives purpose, parts list with stock numbers, and pitching/striking/folding. Same grammar as wall tents, scaled up: more men, more poles, longer ridges; hospital ward and command post tents have stovepipe openings and windows. Do not improvise the sequence on a large tent — mark, spread, pin corners, set ridge and uprights, raise, guy, then wall pins.
