# Advanced Ground Fighting — FM 3-25.150 (2002), Chapter 4

## Advanced attacks (Section I)

- **Advanced body positions (4-1):** refinements of the base positions.
- **Pass the guard (4-2):** the key advanced skill — the guard is the bottom fighter's fortress; passing it is the price of the mount.
- **Attacks from the mount (4-3):** strikes and arm bars from the top.
- **Attacks from the back mount (4-4):** rear naked choke and its counters.
- **Attacks from the guard (4-5):** sweeps, arm bars, and chokes from the bottom.
- **Knee mount (4-6):** one knee in the abdomen, other foot posted — transitional between side control and mount; mobile enough to strike, stable enough to control.
- **Leg attacks (4-7):** ankle locks and knee bars — powerful, fast, dangerous to train; taught late, drilled with control.

## Strikes on the ground (Section II)

- **Passing the guard under strikes (4-8), striking from side control (4-9), defending against strikes in the guard (4-10).**
- Strikes and grappling are one system: strikes open the guard pass, the pass creates the mount, the mount creates the finish.
