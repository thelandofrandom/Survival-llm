# Basic Ground Fighting — FM 3-25.150 (2002), Chapter 3

## Dominant body positions (Section I) — the hierarchy

In order of control:

1. **Back mount** — behind the opponent, both hooks in, control of the upper body. Most dominant position in the system.
2. **Front mount** — astride the face-up opponent's chest or abdomen.
3. **Side control** — perpendicular across the opponent's torso.
4. **Guard** — legs locked around the opponent, controlling from the bottom. Defensive, but a platform for sweeps and submissions.

Every positioning move climbs the hierarchy; every escape descends it.

## Basic techniques (Section II)

- **Body positioning moves (3-5):** movements between positions — achieve the mount from side control, pass the guard, escape the mount, escape the back mount, arm trap and roll. Taught as movements, not static holds; a fight is won in the transitions.
- **Finishing moves (3-6):** rear naked choke; cross-collar choke from mount and guard; bent arm bar from mount and side control; straight arm bar from mount and guard; guillotine choke. Position first, then finish.
- **Drills (3-7):** positioning moves drilled to automaticity.
- **Defense against headlocks (3-8):** the untrained fighter's favorite hold — posture, hand fighting, escape to a dominant position. A basic skill, not an advanced one.
