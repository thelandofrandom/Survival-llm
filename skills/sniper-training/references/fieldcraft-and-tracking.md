# Fieldcraft and Tracking — FM 23-10

## Camouflage Principles

- **Blend:** match color, texture, and shape to the background.
- **Break up the outline:** the human silhouette and straight rifle lines are what the eye catches.
- **Kill shine:** optics, skin, metal — cover or dull everything reflective.
- **Stay still:** movement is detected before shape, shape before color.
- **Use shadow and dead ground:** never skyline on a ridge; move through folds in the ground.

## Ghillie Suit

- Loose, shaggy over-garment on a netting/burlap base, garnished with **local** vegetation from the hide area.
- Garnish is replaced as it wilts — vegetation from one place looks wrong in another.
- The weapon is camouflaged too; a veil over the scope kills glint.
- A pad where the butt contacts the body reduces pulse-beat and breathing transmitted to the weapon.

## Movement

- Slow, deliberate, only when observation allows. Scale the technique to exposure:
  - **Sniper's crawl:** body flat, rifle cradled, inches at a time — final approach under observation.
  - **High/low crawl:** faster, for covered ground.
  - **Walking:** only where cover allows.
- Route selection: dead ground, vegetation, shadow. Never move in the open when a covered route exists.
- Noise discipline and light discipline are absolute: no white light, no talking above a whisper, no metal-on-metal contact.
- Freeze on any sign of detection. A stalk is judged on whether the team is detected, not on speed.

## The Hide Site

Select for: (1) field of fire over the target area; (2) concealment from ground and air; (3) multiple avenues of escape; (4) protection from the environment.

Hide routine:
- Weapon stays in place; optics are handed between team members.
- Data book, observation logs, range cards, and radio sit between the two men, reachable by both.
- Rest, eating, and latrine system arranged in advance; latrine calls leave no sign.
- Occupy before first light, leave after dark. A hasty hide is built in minutes but follows the same logic.

## Observation Method

- **Scan systematically:** slow, overlapping sweeps in sectors, near to far.
- **Naked eye first** — optics narrow the field of view and tire the eye; use the M49 observation telescope for detail after the eye finds something.
- **Record as you go:** the observation log is a written record of what was seen, when, and where. It is the team's intelligence product.
- **What betrays a hidden man:** shine, shape, shadow, silhouette, spacing, movement, color contrast.
- **Kim's game** (sustainment training): study a set of objects, cover them, recall them in detail. Builds the visual memory the observer needs to notice what changed in a scene.

## Target Selection and Reporting

- Priorities are set before the mission: officers, crew-served weapon crews, radio operators, other high-value personnel.
- Any enemy threatening the team's position is a high-value target.
- Report in **SALT** format: Size, Activity, Location, Time — location precise enough for fires or maneuver to act on.

## Tracking

When a sniper follows a trail, he asks: *How many persons am I following? What is their state of training? How are they equipped?*

**Sign to read:** footprints, broken vegetation, displaced stones, litter, scent, disturbed ground, spider webs, dew patterns.

**Aging sign:**
| Age | Indicators |
|---|---|
| Fresh (minutes–hours) | Sharp edges, moist soil, crushed vegetation still green, scent present |
| Recent (hours) | Edges softening, vegetation wilting, soil drying |
| Old (days) | Weathered edges, dry soil, vegetation brown, rain/dew effects |

**Tracker-team drill:** one man reads sign, the other provides security and records. Never outrun security. A trail that gets too easy is a trap until proven otherwise — never follow a trail into an ambush.

## Countertracking

- Move on hard ground; step on rocks and roots; brush out sign; use streams.
- Split up and rejoin; lay false trails; move at night or in rain when sign degrades fastest.
- Countertracking is a mindset applied to every movement: leave nothing, and where you must leave something, make it misleading.
