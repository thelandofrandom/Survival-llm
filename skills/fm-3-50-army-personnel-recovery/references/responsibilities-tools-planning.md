# Responsibilities, Tools, Planning — FM 3-50

## Responsibilities (Chapter 2)

- The commander owns PR; the staff integrates it; every leader from company level up is accountable for the personnel in their charge.
- Responsibilities include: maintaining accountability; producing and disseminating PR guidance and ISG; ensuring SERE training for personnel at risk; maintaining the PR architecture (coordination processes for Army headquarters, Figure 2-1); executing the five tasks when an isolating event occurs.
- Responsibilities are assigned across the Army warfighting functions — PR is not a single staff section's job.

## Supporting Tools

- Joint and Army policy and doctrinal principles, SOPs, execution documents (OPLANs, OPORDs).
- Collaborative software used to conduct Army operations.
- **Personnel Recovery Mission Software (PRMS)** — the website used to obtain and manage ISOPREP data and evasion plans of action.
- **Appendix 13 (Personnel Recovery) to Annex E (Protection)** — the Army plans-and-orders location for PR information (FM 6-0 formats).
- **ISOPREP** (isolated personnel report) and **EPA** (evasion plan of action) — the two documents that turn an anonymous missing person into a known individual with a plan. Keep them current: an out-of-date ISOPREP or a missing EPA is a gap the recovery force discovers at the worst possible moment.

## Categories of Information for PR

The staff must gather and maintain the categories of information for PR — the who, what, where, and how of the recovery problem. Without them, the appendix is a template, not a plan.

## The PR Appendix

- The product of PR planning: the PR appendix to the operation plan (Figure 3-1 gives an example; Army location: Appendix 13 to Annex E).
- It answers, in advance: who is responsible, what forces are available, what the communications and authentication procedures are, what the recovery methods are, what the authorities are.
- Built during mission analysis and refined through the rest of the MDMP.

## Evasion Plans of Action

- Table 3-8 gives sample EPA categories.
- The EPA is the isolated person's plan, written before the mission: what they will do if isolated, where they will go, how they will communicate, how they will authenticate, what the recovery force should expect.
- The EPA and the ISOPREP are managed through PRMS.
