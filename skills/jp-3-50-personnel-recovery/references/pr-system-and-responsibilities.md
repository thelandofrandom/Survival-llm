# PR System and Responsibilities — JP 3-50

## What Personnel Recovery Is

Personnel recovery is the sum of military, diplomatic, and civil efforts to prepare for and execute the recovery and reintegration of isolated personnel. Isolated personnel are military or civilian personnel separated from their unit or in a situation where they must survive, evade, resist, or escape. An isolating event is the event that separates them.

**Purpose:** prevent loss of life, capture, and exploitation. Adversaries have historically exploited captured personnel for intelligence, propaganda, or as leverage during negotiations; PR intends to reduce those opportunities.

## The Four Functions of the DOD PR System

1. **Preparation** — education, training, and equipping of the three elements (commanders and staff, recovery forces, isolated personnel).
2. **Planning** — mission analysis, the basic plan, the PR appendix.
3. **Execution** — the five tasks (below).
4. **Adaptation** — lessons from each event fed back into preparation and planning.

## The Five Execution Tasks

| Task | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Report** | Notification that personnel are or may have become isolated |
| **Locate** | Find and confirm the identity of isolated personnel |
| **Support** | Support to the isolated person and to the next of kin |
| **Recover** | Coordinated actions of commanders/staffs, recovery forces, and isolated personnel |
| **Reintegrate** | Process beginning at report notification, continuing after physical custody |

## Functions and Responsibilities

- **Geographic combatant commanders:** plan for effective and comprehensive PR; establish command relationships and procedures to employ forces from different components jointly; establish a PR coordination architecture; ensure PR-trained personnel fill critical positions.
- **Services and USSOCOM:** each Service normally establishes a PR office of primary responsibility; Service Chiefs and CDRUSSOCOM organize, train, and equip forces for PR and provide forces and processes to accomplish the five tasks, consistent with DOD guidance and OPLANs.
- **Joint Personnel Recovery Agency (JPRA):** the CJCS's controlled activity for PR, working through the Joint Staff Directorate for Joint Force Development; facilitates interoperable PR capabilities through joint PR guidance and PR products.
- **Defense Forensics and Biometric Agency:** supports identification of isolated personnel, including through forensic and biometric means.
- **Joint Personnel Recovery Center (JPRC):** assists the JFC in planning and coordinating PR operations within the assigned operational area — the JFC's PR staff hub.
- **Component commanders and subordinate organizations:** plan, conduct, and designate PR forces for their own operations and for isolating events in their operational area or as tasked by the JFC. Consider capabilities of other components, multinational forces, other U.S. Government departments and agencies, intergovernmental and nongovernmental organizations — and establish habitual relationships before the event.
- **Intelligence activities:** threat assessment, information on isolated personnel, support to the locate task. Intelligence not shared with the PR architecture is wasted.

## Command and Control

- Delineate command relationships and PR coordination authority in JTF and component operation orders and fragmentary orders.
- PR requires coordination with staff elements at the JTF and subordinate levels.
- A component experiencing an isolating event in its operational area may be able to conduct the recovery without external support. If two or more components are involved, the JPRC coordinates augmentation.
- **Communications:** must work when everything else is failing — distress notification, location reporting, authentication, coordination with the recovery force. Plan redundant means, authentication procedures, and procedures for isolated personnel with only improvised means.
- **Coordination and liaison:** PR crosses component, Service, agency, and national boundaries. Liaison — people who know both sides of a boundary — is what makes the architecture work under time pressure. Establish it before the operation.
