# Preparation and Planning — JP 3-50

## Preparation (Chapter IV)

### Command and Policy Guidance

Preparation starts with command guidance: the commander's PR guidance, disseminated to subordinate leaders, sets the standard for what is expected of individuals, units, and staffs. Revise as conditions change.

### Personnel Recovery Education and Training

The three elements of the PR system are prepared through education and training:
- **Isolated personnel** — SERE training appropriate to their risk of isolation.
- **Commanders and staff** — the PR planning and execution process.
- **Recovery forces** — the recovery mission itself.

Training is tiered by risk: not everyone needs the same depth, but everyone at risk needs something.

### Products and Equipment

- Isolated personnel reports (**ISOPREP**) and evasion plans of action (**EPA**) — who the isolated person is and what they intend to do.
- Blood chits and other aids.
- Survival and signaling equipment.
- Communications equipment for calling for help and authenticating.
- Equipment not carried, not maintained, or not trained on is inventory, not preparation.

## Planning (Chapter V)

### Personnel Recovery Mission Analysis

What are the isolation risks in this operation, who is at risk, where, and from what? The analysis drives the PR guidance, the training, the equipment, and the appendix to the plan.

### Communication Synchronization

PR planning must be synchronized with the communications plan: who can talk to whom, on what means, with what authentication, and what happens when the primary means fails. A recovery plan depending on a single communications path has a single point of failure.

### The Basic Plan and the PR Appendix

- The operational concept for PR and the authorities for PR are developed in the **basic plan**.
- Detailed PR content goes in the **personnel recovery appendix to the operation plan** (Appendix 13 to Annex E in Army plans, with the joint equivalent).
- The appendix must answer, in advance, the questions a staff will ask at 0300 when someone is missing: who is responsible, what forces are available, what are the communications and authentication procedures, what are the recovery methods, what are the authorities.
- A staff that has to invent these answers during an event has already lost time it cannot recover.

### PR Mission Planning and Execution

Deliberate work turning the appendix into a specific recovery mission: task organization, routes, communications, authentication, contingency plans, and the decision points governing launch, abort, and recovery.
