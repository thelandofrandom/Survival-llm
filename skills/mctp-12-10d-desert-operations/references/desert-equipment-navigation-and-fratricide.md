# Desert Navigation, Equipment, and Fratricide Reduction

Distilled from MCTP 12-10D (ATP 3-90.99), Chapter 1 and Appendices A–D (Apr 2021).

## Navigation and Refraction

- The desert lies to the eye. **Heat shimmer and mirages** displace apparent positions; **refraction** (Figures 1-5 day, 1-6 night) bends light so objects appear where they are not.
- Distances are systematically misjudged in clear desert air — a hill that looks two miles away may be ten.
- **Correctives:** dead reckoning, compass work, GPS. The eye is not a navigation instrument, day or night.

## Operating Equipment in the Desert (Appendix B)

- **The threat:** heat, dust, and sand attack everything. Engines ingest dust and lose power; lubricants thin and fail; electronics overheat; optics are degraded by dust and heat shimmer; batteries drain faster in heat and fail in cold desert nights.
- **The discipline:** daily cleaning, filters, covers. Appendix B is the techniques manual — the daily routine that keeps vehicles, weapons, and electronics running when the environment is trying to stop them.
- Plan maintenance into the operation the way you plan water and fuel; a vehicle that stops in the desert is a casualty, not an inconvenience.

## Fratricide Reduction (Appendix C)

- The desert's long fields of fire and fast tempo make fratricide a constant risk.
- Countermeasures: identification, coordination, and control measures — the appendix's technique set. In open terrain with weapons that outrange identification, positive ID and disciplined fire control are the only reliable defenses.
- The same speed and openness that favor the offense favor fratricide; the faster the tempo, the tighter the control measures must be.

## Desert Regions of the World (Appendix A)

- The appendix catalogs the notable deserts of the world — their locations and characteristics — as a planning reference. Know which desert you are in before you plan for it: a rocky plateau desert and a sand-sea desert are different battlefields that happen to share a lack of rain.

- Joint Army–Marine Corps publication: Appendix D crosswalks Army and Marine Corps doctrinal terms. When a term differs between services, the appendix is the authority for this publication.
