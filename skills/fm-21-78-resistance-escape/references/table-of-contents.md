## Extraction Notes

The vault PDF is a fax-like scan; the extraction is heavily garbled in places. Page headers in the OCR read "FM 21-79" and "UNCLASSIFIED" — these are OCR artifacts, not source facts; the manual is FM 21-78, edition of 15 June 1989, and the manual itself was classified. Two section titles in Chapter 11 (11-7, 11-8) could not be recovered from the extraction. All other titles above are as read from the extracted text.

Source: FM 21-78, *Resistance and Escape*, Headquarters, Department of the Army, 15 June 1989 (classified), table of contents only, 4 pages. Full text of the vault PDF: `references/full-text.md`.
