# FM 21-78 — Scope and Limitations

## What Is Known

- **The manual:** FM 21-78, *Resistance and Escape*, Headquarters, Department of the Army, edition of 15 June 1989. The manual itself was classified; the vault hosts only its table of contents.
- **The source:** a 4-page, 647-word PDF — the table of contents, posted by Jeff Schmidt, author of *Disciplined Minds*. Cover line: "Resistance and Escape, United States Army field manual FM 21-78, Edition of 15 June 1989 (classified), Table of contents."
- **The scope:** twelve chapters plus five appendices, a glossary, references, and an index (see `references/table-of-contents.md`). The arc runs from the history of POW treatment and the Code of Conduct, through the effects of captivity, organization in captivity, exploitation, propaganda, indoctrination, and interrogation, to resistance techniques, camp communication, escape and rescue, and finally peacetime detention — including terrorist captivity and hostage situations.
- **The appendices:** reading material; an extract of DoD Directive 1300.7 (December 1984); extracted articles of the Geneva Convention on the treatment of POWs; extracted articles of the UCMJ; release policy.

## What Is Not Known

- **No chapter body text exists in this source.** No procedure, technique, definition, or quotation from FM 21-78 can be reproduced from the vault PDF, because the PDF contains only the table of contents.
- **Chapter 11, sections 11-7 and 11-8:** the section titles are illegible in the extraction and cannot be recovered from this source.
- **The manual's classification level and distribution** beyond the cover's "(classified)" marking are not stated in the source.
- **Whether the chapter titles changed** between the 1989 edition and any later revision is not established by this source.

## How to Use This Skill

This skill is a map, not the manual. Use it to answer "what does FM 21-78 cover?" and to route a question to the skill that holds the actual doctrine:

- **Resistance, escape, and SERE doctrine (current):** `sere` (AFH 10-644, *SERE Operations*, 2017).
- **Survival, evasion, and recovery (Marine Corps):** `survival-evasion-recovery` (MCRP 3-02H).
- **The 1958 predecessor, full text:** `fm-21-77-evasion-escape` (FM 21-77, May 1958) — the Army's first comprehensive evasion and escape manual, with complete chapters on the Code of Conduct, short- and long-range evasion, POW camp life, interrogation resistance, indoctrination, and escape from camp. Note that FM 21-77 is historic Cold War doctrine, not a substitute for FM 21-78's 1989 content.
- **The Code of Conduct itself:** the six articles are reproduced in full in `fm-21-77-evasion-escape` (SKILL.md §2 and `../../fm-21-77-evasion-escape/references/code-of-conduct-pow-camp.md`).

## What Not to Do

- Do not infer chapter content from chapter titles and present it as FM 21-78 doctrine.
- Do not present FM 21-77 material as if it were FM 21-78.
- Do not guess at the illegible Chapter 11 section titles.
- Do not treat the OCR artifacts ("FM 21-79," "UNCLASSIFIED" page headers) as source facts.
