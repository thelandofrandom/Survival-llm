# Radio, Signaling, and Recovery — FM 21-76-1

## Radio Communications (Chapter III)

- Use the radio correctly and sparingly — every transmission can be located.
- Follow the procedures in your plan and orders: authenticate, report concisely, follow recovery-force instructions.
- Keep transmissions short; listen more than you talk.
- If you have no radio, visual and sound signals are your only means of effecting recovery.

## Signaling

- **Signaling mirror** — most effective daytime visual signal.
- **Smoke** by day; **fire** by night.
- **Ground-to-air signals** — large, contrasting symbols in open ground.
- **Sound** — whistle blasts in groups of three; gunshots.
- Signal only when ready to be found and when the risk is acceptable — in hostile areas a signal is as visible to the enemy as to rescue.
- Prepare signaling devices before you need them; keep at least one on your body.

## What to Report to the Recovery Force

1. Enemy activity in the recovery area.
2. Recovery-site characteristics (slope, obstacles, size).
3. Number in party / medical situation.
4. Signal devices available.

## Recovery Site Selection (Chapter IV)

- Locate an area for landing pick-up if practical: approximately **150 feet diameter, free of obstructions, flat and level**.
- Assess evidence of human activity at/near the site (in combat).
- Locate several concealment sites around the area (in combat).
- Plan several tactical entry and exit routes (in combat).

## Site Preparation

1. Pack and secure all equipment.
2. Prepare signaling devices (use as directed or as briefed).
3. Mentally review recovery methods (aircraft, ground, boat).

## Recovery Procedures

- Be prepared to **authenticate** as directed in applicable plans/orders.
- Follow recovery-force instructions exactly at pickup: approach the aircraft only as directed; keep clear of rotors and propellers; do not move toward the aircraft until told to.
- After physical custody, follow the recovery force's directions — the evasion is over; reintegration begins.
- **Authentication:** how the recovery force confirms you are who you claim to be, and how you confirm they are friendly. Use the procedures in your plan and orders (challenge and reply, codes, or other means as briefed). Never authenticate to an unknown party; never board an aircraft or vehicle that has not authenticated itself.
