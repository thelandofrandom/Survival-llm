# Evasion and Navigation — FM 21-76-1

## Quick Reference Checklist (Front of Manual)

**Decide to Survive!**

- **S — Size up the situation,** surroundings, physical condition, equipment.
- **U — Use all your senses.**
- **R — Remember where you are.**
- **V — Vanquish fear and panic.**
- **I — Improvise.**
- **V — Value living.**
- **A — Act like the natives.**
- **L — Live by your wits, but for now, learn basic skills.**

## Immediate Actions

1. Find a hole-up site.
2. Reassess situation; treat injuries, then inventory equipment.
3. Review plan of action; establish priorities.
4. Determine current location.
5. Improve camouflage.
6. Focus thoughts on the task(s) at hand.
7. Execute plan of action. Stay flexible!

## Concealment

Select a place of concealment providing:
- Adequate concealment, ground and air.
- Safe distance from enemy positions and lines of communication.
- Room to rest without being found; no visible trail in or out.
- Improve with natural materials; break up outline; no shiny objects, strong smells, or skyline positions.

## Shelters in Evasion

Low, small, inconspicuous — poncho shelter or natural-material hide. Keep the profile low, camouflage with local materials, be ready to abandon it fast. A shelter visible from the air is worse than no shelter.

## Evasion Movement

- Slow, deliberate, mostly at night.
- Move from concealment to concealment; avoid trails, roads, open ground.
- Cross danger areas quickly; stop frequently to listen and observe.
- Vary direction; never move in a straight line toward the objective for long.
- Use terrain for cover; never silhouette yourself.
- If compromised: break contact, move away fast, find a new hole-up site before the enemy fixes your position.

## Stay or Move?

**Stay** if injured; if your position is known to friendly forces; if movement would expose you to the enemy; if you lack the means to navigate.
**Move** if your position is compromised; if water or food is unavailable; if friendly lines are reachable; if a recovery site is attainable. A wrong move is worse than no move.

## Navigation and Position Determination

- **Compass first** — the primary tool, carried on the body at all times.
- **Sun:** rises roughly east, sets roughly west.
- **Stick-and-shadow:** mark a shadow tip, wait, mark again — the line runs west to east.
- **Watch method:** hour hand at the sun; halfway between the hour hand and 12 o'clock is south (Northern Hemisphere).
- **Stars:** Polaris in the north; the Southern Cross in the south.
- **Position:** terrain association, resection, dead reckoning — keep a running estimate of where you are even when not moving.

## Travel Considerations

- Plan routes using cover and concealment; avoid built-up areas, roads, bridges in hostile territory.
- **River travel:** fast and quiet but exposed, with limited escape routes — use only when the risk is acceptable.
- **Ice and snow:** footing, avalanche terrain, cold injury.
- **Mountains:** falls, rockfall, sudden weather.
- **Summer:** heat and dehydration.
- **Dry climates:** water discipline, night movement.
