# Signaling and Recovery — MCRP 3-02H

## Communications Rules

- Communicate as directed in applicable plans/orders, particularly when considering transmitting in the blind.
- Be prepared to use communications and signaling devices on short notice.
- **Use of communications and signaling devices may compromise your position.** Every transmission trades information for exposure; the theater plan decides the trade.

## Radio

- Use the survival radio per the evasion plan of action and the theater recovery plan.
- Authentication procedures are prearranged — an evader who cannot authenticate will not be recovered by radio.
- Keep transmissions short; transmit only when the plan directs it.

## Visual Signals

| Signal | Use |
|---|---|
| Signal mirror | Use as directed by recovery forces; if no radio, use only with confirmed friendly forces; cover when not in use; sweep the horizon with it |
| Smoke | Black smoke (rubber/oil) against light backgrounds; white smoke (green vegetation) against dark backgrounds; use as directed |
| Panel signals | Prearranged ground panels to mark position and convey simple messages |
| Ground-to-air signals | Standard international ground signals for aircraft |
| Audio (voice, whistle, weapons fire) | When visual signals will not work, and sparingly — sound carries to the enemy too |

## Recovery-Site Selection

Select sites in accordance with theater recovery plan criteria:
- Site free of hazards; personal gear secured.
- Best area for communications and signaling devices.
- Observe the site for proximity to enemy activity and LOC.
- **Follow recovery force instructions** — exactly, immediately, without improvisation.

## Helicopter Recovery

- Stay in position until directed; never approach from the tail.
- Secure all loose equipment before the aircraft arrives.
- Use the hoist or ladder as directed; keep hands and feet clear until told to move.

## Authentication

- Recovery depends on authentication in both directions: the evader proves identity to the recovery force, and the recovery force proves itself to the evader.
- Prearranged codes, questions, and signals are the mechanism.
- An evader who breaks authentication discipline can be recovered by the enemy instead.
